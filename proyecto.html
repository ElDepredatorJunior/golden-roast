Honduran Polytechnic University

University Entrepreneurship Initiative: Golden Roast

Comayagua Associated Center · Academic documentation on research objectives, prototype formulation, value proposition, and technical considerations.

Founding Members: Joselin Cáceres, Sofia López, Ángel Guerra, Génesis López

1. Project Introduction

Golden Roast is an academic business initiative developed around formulating high-value personal care products inspired by natural ingredients such as coffee and pure honey. Our core proposal focuses on skincare through two primary hero products: a coffee-based scrub and a honey-inspired moisturizer, formulated with complementary ingredients designed to keep skin soft, hydrated, and protected.

This business presentation aims to introduce our venture concept, demonstrate working prototypes, and explain how both products integrate into an essential skincare regimen. We also emphasize aesthetic elegance, rigorous ingredient selection, physiological skin safety, and building consumer trust.

With Golden Roast, we transform a simple concept into an entrepreneurial opportunity, emphasizing the value of innovation, creativity, and personal care.

2. Entrepreneurship Objectives

General Objective:

To develop and present an entrepreneurship proposal centered on a coffee exfoliant and a honey-inspired moisturizer, highlighting their features, ingredients, uses, and commercial potential as personal care products.

Specific Objectives:

  • Present two skincare product prototypes: a coffee exfoliant and a honey moisturizer.
  • Describe the characteristics, applications, qualities, and ingredients of each formulated product.
  • Encourage entrepreneurial interest through a creative, attractive proposal focused on personal wellness.
  • Identify strategic opportunities to enhance product presentation, formulation, and commercialization across the central region of Honduras.

3. Prototype Formulation & Safety Assessment

Prototype 1: Coffee Exfoliant

Ingredients: Ground roasted coffee, organic sugar, pure bee honey, virgin coconut oil.

Mechanism of action: Gentle superficial mechanical exfoliation using coffee microparticles and sugar crystals dissolved in a coconut lipid phase. Removes dead surface cells from the stratum corneum while stimulating superficial skin microcirculation.

Prototype 2: Honey Moisturizer

Ingredients: 2 tablespoons pure honey, 4 tablespoons aloe vera gel, 1 teaspoon coconut oil, 1/2 teaspoon vegetable glycerin.

Mechanism of action: Humectant emulsion that provides direct aqueous hydration via aloe vera, transepidermal moisture retention mediated by honey and glycerin, and lipid barrier sealing from coconut triglycerides.

Dermatological Safety Review & Physiological pH (Baking Soda Exclusion):
During preliminary formulation trials, the addition of sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) was evaluated for the exfoliating mixture. Following dermocosmetic standards and the physiological pH of human skin (ranging between 4.7 and 5.5), baking soda (with an alkaline pH near 9) was strictly excluded, preventing disruption of the protective acid mantle and avoiding dryness or irritation in sensitive skin.

4. Project Conclusions & Outlook

The Golden Roast project demonstrates the viability of transforming emblematic Honduran raw materials — such as specialty coffee and pure honey — into artisan cosmetic products with high added value and strong visual appeal.

  • The synergy between the exfoliant and the moisturizer creates a coherent two-step skincare routine (renewal and deep hydration).
  • Brand design and visual identity reinforce the perception of quality, building consumer trust.
  • The implementation of this digital platform establishes the technical foundation for future formal commercialization in the Honduran market.
