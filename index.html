Botanical Synergy

How the Golden Roast Routine Works

Effective skincare does not require unnecessary complexity. Our proposal is built upon two harmonious steps.

1
Step 1: Exfoliation & Renewal

Renew with Coffee Particles

Apply the scrub to damp skin with gentle circular motions. The coffee and sugar particles remove dead surface skin cells through mechanical action.

  • Controlled friction stimulates skin surface microcirculation.
  • Virgin coconut oil and pure honey condition and prevent post-cleanse dryness.
2
Step 2: Hydration & Sealing

Nourish with Honey & Aloe Vera

Once skin is clean and receptive after exfoliation, apply the moisturizer. Pure honey and plant glycerin attract water while aloe vera calms and cools.

  • Sustained moisture retention in superficial skin layers.
  • Velvety, soft touch without greasy or sticky residue.
